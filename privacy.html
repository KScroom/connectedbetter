Privacy policy

This site is a front-end demo of ConnectED, the student platform based in Doha. It explains what this demo does with information you type in.

What stays on your device

The sign-up and log-in forms check your entries in the browser. Passwords are not stored and are not sent to a server. Closing the page clears them.

Support email

The support form opens your email app with a message addressed to connected.qaa@gmail.com. Your mail provider delivers that message. Include only what support needs to help you.

The live product

Accounts on connectedqa.com are a separate system. This demo does not read or write those accounts. For the live product’s privacy practices, contact connected.qaa@gmail.com.

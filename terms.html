Terms of service

ConnectED is a free student platform for finding scholarships, universities, open days, and internships, then tracking applications from one profile.

This website

  • There is no payment, plan, or checkout on this site.
  • Creating a profile here does not open an account on connectedqa.com.
  • Opportunity cards shown publicly are the two examples from the ConnectED homepage. The full catalogue opens after you have an account on the live product.

Students

Sign-up asks you to confirm that a parent or guardian knows about the registration and agrees to these terms. A partnered-school badge, when you add a school, still needs counselor approval before you apply.

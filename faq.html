getting started

what connected is and what it costs.

a platform for ambitious students. discover fully funded scholarships, top-tier universities, open days, and exclusive internships. build one profile and track applications from one dashboard.

no. there is no payment, no checkout, and no paid plan. connected is free to start. you can create a profile and explore the dashboard without platform charges.

create your profile, find high-fit options, apply with context, and track every outcome. guided setup walks you through them.

matching and applications

how the product finds the fit and keeps you on time.

scholarships, open days, and internships in the catalogue, plus universities in the global university hub. search, filter, save, like, and compare.

you complete academic details, preferences, activities, and interests. connected uses that profile to surface scholarships and programs that fit your goals.

academic, personal, and preference fields, plus documents, certificates, activities, and interests. profile readiness shows what is missing before you submit, and you reuse the same profile on every application.

the dashboard keeps application statuses, interviews, decisions, and offers together on a kanban board, with alerts for upcoming deadlines and interview dates.

accounts and support

signup rules and how to reach us.

school is optional. selecting a partnered school gives you a verified badge, and your school counselor approves it before you can apply to opportunities.

email connected.qaa@gmail.com, or use instagram and linkedin. replies usually come within one business day. for urgent account issues, include the email you sign in with.
